## Blocker 1: Parking handoff and marker loss — cleared

The Parking sign is only a visual cue that the bay is ahead. The final parking condition still depends on the camera-detected ArUco marker and the marker-relative pose. During the latest run, the marker was visible during the parking handoff but was later lost before the vehicle reached the required final pose.

The current controller has a short last-pose grace period and a slow Parking-sign fallback. When both the marker and sign are unavailable, it stops rather than continuing blindly. That is safe, but it can leave the vehicle short of the bay.

### Applied fix

1. Port the updated Task 2C staged crossing/straightening controller and steering smoothing.
2. Continue with the last filtered parking command through marker gaps.
3. Finish from the last measured Task 2C forward distance when the marker disappears after the bay midpoint.
4. Validate on the official Hard scene; the final run reached `DONE`.
