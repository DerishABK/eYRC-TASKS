1. Hospital stop now requires two close sign frames, starts slowing on a nearer approach cue, and uses a later image-position threshold than the old trigger that missed the pickup by 1.12 m.
2. Speed 40 commands are capped at 1.05 rad/s for actuator margin; sign presence must be absent for 60 frames before another sign can be counted.
3. Obstacle tracks persist across larger image gaps and count as passed only after repeated sightings and near-camera movement.
4. Parking completion target moved from 0.19 m to 0.10 m forward, matching the 9.2 cm shortfall in the portal review, while preserving lateral and heading checks.
5. Base route speed is being tuned up to the 3.0 rad/s task limit, with curve and cross-track speed scaling. Keep the Speed 40 command capped at 1.05 rad/s.
6. A completed lane change now re-arms after the target lane is acquired and a short settling interval. It no longer waits for obstacle disappearance before it can react to a new obstacle in the current lane.
